Quick

Avocado Toast with Poached Eggs

Creamy avocado spread on whole grain toast topped with perfectly poached eggs and chili flakes.

Ingredients

  • 2 slices whole grain bread
  • 1 ripe avocado
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1/2 tsp chili flakes
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Preparation

  1. Toast bread until golden and crispy.
  2. Mash avocado with lemon juice, salt, and pepper.
  3. Poach eggs in simmering water for 3-4 minutes.
  4. Spread avocado on toast, top with eggs and chili flakes.
Meal Prep

Overnight Oats with Berries

Creamy oats soaked overnight with chia seeds, topped with fresh mixed berries and honey.

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup rolled oats
  • 1 cup almond milk
  • 1 tbsp chia seeds
  • 1 cup mixed berries
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1/4 tsp vanilla extract

Preparation

  1. Mix oats, almond milk, chia seeds, and vanilla in a jar.
  2. Stir well and refrigerate overnight.
  3. In the morning, top with fresh berries.
  4. Drizzle honey and serve chilled.

Greek Yogurt Smoothie Bowl

A thick, creamy yogurt bowl loaded with granola, sliced bananas, and fresh fruit toppings.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup Greek yogurt
  • 1/2 cup frozen mango chunks
  • 1 banana, sliced
  • 1/4 cup granola
  • 1 tbsp almond butter
  • 1 tbsp flaxseeds

Preparation

  1. Blend yogurt and frozen mango until smooth and thick.
  2. Pour into a bowl.
  3. Top with banana slices, granola, and flaxseeds.
  4. Drizzle almond butter and serve immediately.

Veggie Egg Muffins

Fluffy baked egg cups loaded with colorful vegetables — perfect for grab-and-go mornings.

Ingredients

  • 6 large eggs
  • 1/2 cup diced bell peppers
  • 1/4 cup diced onion
  • 1/2 cup baby spinach
  • 1/4 cup shredded cheese
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Preparation

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F and grease a muffin tin.
  2. Whisk eggs with salt and pepper.
  3. Divide veggies into muffin cups and pour egg mixture over.
  4. Top with cheese and bake for 18-20 minutes.
Gluten-Free

Banana Oat Pancakes

Fluffy two-ingredient pancakes made with ripe bananas and oats — naturally sweet and filling.

Ingredients

  • 2 ripe bananas
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/2 cup rolled oats
  • 1/2 tsp cinnamon
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • Fresh fruit to serve

Preparation

  1. Blend bananas, eggs, oats, cinnamon, and baking powder.
  2. Heat a non-stick pan over medium heat.
  3. Pour small circles and cook 2-3 minutes per side.
  4. Serve with fresh fruit or maple syrup.
Vegan

Chia Seed Pudding

A rich, creamy pudding made with chia seeds and coconut milk, topped with mango and kiwi.

Ingredients

  • 3 tbsp chia seeds
  • 1 cup coconut milk
  • 1 tbsp maple syrup
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1/2 mango, diced
  • 1 kiwi, sliced

Preparation

  1. Mix chia seeds, coconut milk, maple syrup, and vanilla.
  2. Stir well and refrigerate for at least 4 hours.
  3. Stir again before serving.
  4. Top with mango and kiwi slices.

Spinach & Feta Omelette

A fluffy omelette filled with wilted spinach, crumbled feta, and fresh herbs for a savory start.

Ingredients

  • 3 large eggs
  • 1 cup baby spinach
  • 1/4 cup crumbled feta cheese
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 1 tbsp fresh dill
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Preparation

  1. Whisk eggs with salt and pepper.
  2. Melt butter in a pan over medium heat.
  3. Pour in eggs and cook until just set.
  4. Add spinach, feta, and dill; fold and serve.

Acai Berry Smoothie Bowl

A vibrant purple acai bowl packed with berries, coconut flakes, and crunchy granola.

Ingredients

  • 1 packet frozen acai puree
  • 1/2 cup frozen blueberries
  • 1/2 banana
  • 1/4 cup almond milk
  • 1/4 cup granola
  • 2 tbsp coconut flakes

Preparation

  1. Blend acai, blueberries, banana, and almond milk until thick.
  2. Pour into a bowl — it should be spoonable, not drinkable.
  3. Top with granola and coconut flakes.
  4. Add fresh fruit and serve immediately.
Comfort

Whole Wheat French Toast

Golden whole wheat French toast with cinnamon egg batter, served with fresh strawberries and yogurt.

Ingredients

  • 4 slices whole wheat bread
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/4 cup milk
  • 1/2 tsp cinnamon
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • Fresh strawberries to serve

Preparation

  1. Whisk eggs, milk, cinnamon, and vanilla together.
  2. Dip bread slices into the egg mixture.
  3. Cook on a buttered pan over medium heat until golden on both sides.
  4. Serve with fresh strawberries and a dollop of yogurt.
