No-Bake

Chocolate Avocado Mousse

Silky, rich chocolate mousse made with ripe avocados, cacao powder, and maple syrup — zero guilt required.

Ingredients

  • 2 ripe avocados
  • 3 tbsp raw cacao powder
  • 3 tbsp maple syrup
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • Pinch of sea salt
  • 2 tbsp almond milk

Preparation

  1. Blend all ingredients until completely smooth.
  2. Taste and adjust sweetness with more maple syrup.
  3. Spoon into glasses and chill for 20 minutes.
  4. Top with berries or cacao nibs and serve.
Frozen

Banana Ice Cream

Creamy one-ingredient ice cream from frozen bananas — endlessly customizable with your favorite toppings.

Ingredients

  • 4 ripe bananas, frozen
  • 2 tbsp Almond milk
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • Toppings: granola, berries, honey

Preparation

  1. Slice and freeze bananas overnight.
  2. Blend frozen bananas until smooth and creamy.
  3. Mix in peanut butter and cinnamon if using.
  4. Serve immediately or freeze for a firmer texture.
Raw

Berry Chia Pudding

Overnight chia pudding layered with fresh mixed berries, coconut milk, and a drizzle of honey.

Ingredients

  • 3 tbsp chia seeds
  • 1 cup coconut milk
  • 1 tbsp honey or maple syrup
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 cup mixed berries

Preparation

  1. Stir chia seeds into coconut milk with vanilla and honey.
  2. Refrigerate for at least 4 hours or overnight.
  3. Stir well before serving.
  4. Layer with fresh berries and serve chilled.
No-Bake

Oat & Date Energy Balls

Chewy, naturally sweet bites packed with rolled oats, medjool dates, and dark chocolate chips.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup rolled oats
  • 10 medjool dates, pitted
  • 2 tbsp almond butter
  • 2 tbsp dark chocolate chips
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • Pinch of sea salt

Preparation

  1. Blend dates into a paste in a food processor.
  2. Mix in oats, almond butter, vanilla, and salt.
  3. Fold in chocolate chips by hand.
  4. Roll into balls and refrigerate for 30 minutes.
Baked

Baked Cinnamon Pears

Tender halved pears roasted with cinnamon, a drizzle of honey, and topped with crushed walnuts.

Ingredients

  • 3 ripe pears, halved and cored
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 1/4 cup chopped walnuts
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
  • Greek yogurt, to serve

Preparation

  1. Place pear halves cut-side up on a baking sheet.
  2. Drizzle with honey, vanilla, and dust with cinnamon.
  3. Bake at 375°F for 20–25 minutes until tender.
  4. Top with walnuts and a dollop of Greek yogurt.
Frozen

Mango Coconut Sorbet

A tropical two-ingredient sorbet blended from frozen mango and creamy coconut milk — vegan and refreshing.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups frozen mango chunks
  • 1/3 cup coconut milk
  • 1 tbsp lime juice
  • 1 tsp lime zest
  • Pinch of sea salt

Preparation

  1. Blend frozen mango with coconut milk until smooth.
  2. Add lime juice, zest, and salt; blend again.
  3. Serve immediately as soft-serve or freeze 1 hour for firmer texture.
  4. Scoop and garnish with fresh mint.
Frozen

Greek Yogurt Bark

Frozen Greek yogurt topped with colorful berries, granola, and a honey drizzle — snappy and satisfying.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups plain Greek yogurt
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1/2 cup sliced strawberries
  • 1/4 cup blueberries
  • 2 tbsp granola
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract

Preparation

  1. Mix yogurt with vanilla and 1 tbsp honey.
  2. Spread onto a parchment-lined baking sheet.
  3. Scatter berries and granola on top; drizzle with remaining honey.
  4. Freeze for 3–4 hours, then break into pieces.
Baked

Almond Flour Brownies

Fudgy, gluten-free brownies made with almond flour, coconut sugar, and rich dark chocolate — dense and decadent.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup almond flour
  • 1/3 cup coconut sugar
  • 1/4 cup cacao powder
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/4 cup coconut oil, melted
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract

Preparation

  1. Whisk eggs, coconut oil, vanilla, and sugar together.
  2. Fold in almond flour and cacao powder until combined.
  3. Pour into a greased 8×8 pan.
  4. Bake at 350°F for 18–20 minutes; cool before cutting.

Strawberry Smoothie Bowl

A thick, vibrant strawberry base loaded with fresh fruit, seeds, coconut flakes, and a crunch of granola.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups frozen strawberries
  • 1 frozen banana
  • 1/4 cup almond milk
  • Toppings: kiwi, granola, coconut flakes, chia seeds

Preparation

  1. Blend frozen strawberries, banana, and almond milk until thick and smooth.
  2. Add more milk one tablespoon at a time if needed.
  3. Pour into a bowl.
  4. Arrange toppings decoratively and serve immediately.
