One-Pan

One-Pan Lemon Herb Chicken

Juicy chicken baked with lemon, rosemary, and tender vegetables for an easy dinner.

Ingredients

  • 2 chicken breasts
  • 1 lemon, sliced
  • 1 cup baby carrots
  • 1 cup baby potatoes
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp dried rosemary

Preparation

  1. Toss chicken and vegetables with oil, lemon, and rosemary.
  2. Arrange on a baking sheet in one layer.
  3. Bake at 400°F for 25-30 minutes.
  4. Serve hot with pan juices.
Seafood

Garlic Shrimp & Veggie Skillet

Quick garlic shrimp cooked with zucchini, tomatoes, and spinach for a light dinner.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb shrimp, peeled
  • 1 zucchini, sliced
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes
  • 2 cups spinach
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp olive oil

Preparation

  1. Sauté garlic in olive oil until fragrant.
  2. Add shrimp and cook until pink.
  3. Add zucchini and tomatoes, cooking until tender.
  4. Stir in spinach until wilted and serve.
Bowl

Turkey Taco Salad Bowl

A flavorful salad bowl with seasoned turkey, beans, avocado, and crisp greens.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground turkey
  • 1 can black beans, drained
  • 4 cups mixed greens
  • 1 avocado, sliced
  • 1/2 cup salsa
  • 1 tsp taco seasoning

Preparation

  1. Cook turkey with taco seasoning until browned.
  2. Toss greens with beans and salsa.
  3. Top with turkey and avocado.
  4. Serve with lime wedges.
Balanced

Roasted Veggie Buddha Bowl

A filling bowl with roasted vegetables, quinoa, chickpeas, and tahini dressing.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup cooked quinoa
  • 1 cup roasted Brussels sprouts
  • 1 cup roasted sweet potatoes
  • 1/2 cup chickpeas
  • 2 tbsp tahini
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice

Preparation

  1. Arrange quinoa, veggies, and chickpeas in a bowl.
  2. Whisk tahini with lemon juice.
  3. Drizzle dressing over the bowl.
  4. Serve warm or at room temperature.
Stir-Fry

Beef & Broccoli Stir Fry

A simple stir fry with lean beef, broccoli, and a savory ginger soy sauce.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb flank steak, sliced
  • 3 cups broccoli florets
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • 1 garlic clove, minced
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil

Preparation

  1. Stir fry beef in sesame oil until browned.
  2. Add garlic, ginger, and broccoli.
  3. Pour in soy sauce and cook until broccoli is tender.
  4. Serve over brown rice.
Comfort

Creamy Mushroom Farro

A comforting, easy grain bowl with farro, mushrooms, spinach, and garlic.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup cooked farro
  • 8 oz mushrooms, sliced
  • 2 cups spinach
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1/4 cup vegetable broth
  • 2 tbsp Parmesan

Preparation

  1. Sauté mushrooms and garlic until golden.
  2. Add spinach and cook until wilted.
  3. Stir in farro and broth until heated.
  4. Finish with Parmesan and serve.
Soup

Lentil Tomato Stew

A hearty lentil stew with tomatoes, carrots, and warming spices for a comforting meal.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup red lentils
  • 1 can diced tomatoes
  • 1 carrot, diced
  • 1 celery stalk, diced
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 4 cups vegetable broth

Preparation

  1. Sauté carrots and celery until softened.
  2. Add lentils, tomatoes, and broth.
  3. Simmer until lentils are tender.
  4. Season with paprika and serve warm.
Vegetarian

Sweet Potato & Black Bean Enchiladas

Warm enchiladas filled with seasoned sweet potato, black beans, and topped with avocado.

Ingredients

  • 2 sweet potatoes, cubed
  • 1 can black beans, drained
  • 8 corn tortillas
  • 1 cup enchilada sauce
  • 1 avocado, sliced
  • 1 tsp chili powder

Preparation

  1. Roast sweet potatoes with chili powder.
  2. Mix with black beans and fill tortillas.
  3. Place in a baking dish and top with enchilada sauce.
  4. Bake at 375°F for 15 minutes and garnish with avocado.
Asian

Thai Basil Chicken

A fragrant stir fry with ground chicken, basil, bell pepper, and a tangy sauce.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground chicken
  • 1 red bell pepper, sliced
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp fish sauce
  • 1 cup fresh basil leaves

Preparation

  1. Cook garlic and chicken until browned.
  2. Add bell pepper and sauces.
  3. Stir in basil until wilted.
  4. Serve with steamed rice.
Vegan

Chickpea & Spinach Curry

A creamy curry with chickpeas, spinach, tomatoes, and warming Indian spices.

Ingredients

  • 1 can chickpeas, drained
  • 3 cups spinach
  • 1 can diced tomatoes
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tsp curry powder

Preparation

  1. Sauté onion and garlic until soft.
  2. Add tomatoes, chickpeas, and curry powder.
  3. Simmer until flavors meld.
  4. Stir in spinach and cook until wilted.
