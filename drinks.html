Berry Oat Smoothie

A refreshing blend of fresh berries and creamy oats for a healthy breakfast or snack.

Ingredients

  • 2 cup frozen mixed berries
  • 1 cup rolled oats
  • 1 cup almond milk
  • 1 tablespoon honey

Preparation

  1. Combine all ingredients in a blender and blend until smooth.
  2. Pour into glasses and serve immediately.

Ginger Green Tea

A warming blend of fresh ginger and green tea for a soothing beverage.

Ingredients

  • 1 green tea bag
  • 1 inch fresh ginger, sliced
  • 1 cup hot water

Preparation

  1. Place the green tea bag and ginger slices in a cup.
  2. Pour hot water over the ingredients.
  3. Steep for 3-5 minutes.
  4. Remove the tea bag and ginger slices.
  5. Serve hot.

Tropical Green Detox

a refreshing blend of tropical fruits and green vegetables for a revitalizing drink.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup fresh spinach
  • 1 cup pineapple chunks
  • 4 cup coconut water
  • 1 cup cucumber, diced
  • 1/2 squeez of fresh lemon

Preparation

  1. Combine all ingredients in a blender and blend until smooth.
  2. Pour into glasses and serve immediately.

lemon ginger juice

A refreshing blend of lemon and ginger for a soothing drink.

Ingredients

  • 1 liter of cold water
  • 1 lemon, thinly sliced
  • 1 inch fresh ginger, sliced
  • 1/2 fresh mint leaves

Preparation

  1. add lemon, ginger, and mint to the cold water.
  2. fill with water and stir well.
  3. refrigerate for at least 30 minutes before serving.

beetroot Apple juice

A refreshing blend of beetroot and apple for a healthy drink.

Ingredients

  • 1 medium beetroot, peeled and chopped
  • 2 green apples
  • 1 large carrots
  • 1 inch fresh ginger, sliced

Preparation

  1. wash and chop all the vegetables and fruits
  2. pass all through a juicer
  3. serve chilled over ice if desired
