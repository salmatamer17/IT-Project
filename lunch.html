Meat

Lemon herb roasted chicken

A high-protein, low-carb meal packed with fresh Mediterranean flavors.

Ingredients

  • chicken breasts or thighs.
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil, 3 cloves of minced garlic, and fresh lemon juice.
  • Fresh Rosemary and Thyme.
  • Mixed vegetables (Broccoli, carrots, and baby potatoes).

Preparation

  1. Marinate the chicken with garlic, lemon, olive oil, and herbs for 20 minutes.
  2. Place the chicken and chopped vegetables on a baking tray.
  3. Roast in a preheated oven at 200°C for 30-35 minutes until golden brown.
Seafood

Grilled Salmon & Quinoa Power Bowl

Rich in Omega-3 and fiber, perfect for a balanced healthy lunch.

Ingredients

  • Fresh Salmon fillet.
  • 1/2 cup cooked Quinoa.
  • Sliced Avocado and cherry tomatoes.
  • Dressing: Honey mustard and a splash of apple cider vinegar.

Preparation

  1. Season the salmon with salt and pepper, then grill for 4-5 minutes per side.
  2. Boil quinoa in water (1:2 ratio) until fluffy.
  3. Assemble the bowl starting with quinoa, then add the grilled salmon, avocado, and drizzle with dressing.
Salad

Roasted Sweet Potato & Chickpea Salad

A delicious vegan-friendly lunch that is both filling and nutritious.

Ingredients

  • 1 can of Chickpeas (rinsed and drained).
  • 2 medium Sweet potatoes (cubed).
  • Fresh Baby spinach or Arugula.
  • Tahini dressing (Tahini, lemon juice, and a pinch of cumin).

Preparation

  1. Toss sweet potato cubes in olive oil and roast at 200°C for 20 minutes.
  2. In a large bowl, mix the roasted potatoes with chickpeas and spinach.
  3. Pour the creamy tahini dressing over the salad and toss well before serving.
Vegetarian

Creamy Avocado Pasta

A healthy, dairy-free take on pasta using avocado for a rich, creamy texture.

Ingredients

  • Whole wheat or gluten-free Spaghetti.
  • 1 Ripe Avocado, fresh basil leaves, and 2 cloves of garlic.
  • Cherry tomatoes (for topping).
  • Cherry tomatoes (for topping).s

Preparation

  1. Boil the pasta according to package instructions.
  2. Blend the avocado, basil, garlic, and lemon juice until smooth.
  3. Toss the pasta with the avocado sauce and top with fresh cherry tomatoes.
Seafood

Garlic Butter Shrimp with Asparagus

A quick, low-calorie seafood dish that looks and tastes like a 5-star meal.

Ingredients

  • Large Shrimp (peeled and deveined).
  • A bunch of fresh Asparagus.
  • 1 tbsp Butter (or Ghee), lemon zest, and red pepper flakes.
  • Fresh parsley.

Preparation

  1. SSauté asparagus in a pan with a bit of butter until tender.
  2. Add the shrimp and garlic, cooking for 2-3 minutes per side.
  3. Finish with lemon zest and chopped parsley.
Vegetarian

Mediterranean Quinoa Salad with Feta

A refreshing salad that works perfectly as a light lunch or a side dish.

Ingredients

  • Cooked Quinoa.
  • Cucumber, red onion, and kalamata olives.
  • Low-fat Feta cheese.
  • Dressing: Red wine vinegar and dried oregano.

Preparation

  1. Chop all vegetables into small cubes.
  2. In a large bowl, combine quinoa, vegetables, olives, and crumbled feta.
  3. Whisk the dressing ingredients and pour over the salad.
